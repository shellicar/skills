# interlocutor: editorial context

Not loaded at runtime. Read before you change `ROLE.md`. The reasoning that shapes behaviour lives in the ROLE; this holds the history, the decisions, and what was rejected.

## Where it came from

The first role of the mission pipeline — the one the handler is before anything is built, drawing the SC's intent out. It replaced two earlier attempts: `requirements-analyst` (a checklist of artefacts to produce, which made "produce a thorough agreement" the goal, so it fabricated to look thorough) and `confidant` (right instinct — the work happens in the conversation — but mis-scoped as the whole of planning, and its name carried a therapy prior that leaked "press on a soft spot").

## Decisions

- SAM position: carried / sc / prose. Substance measures the deliverable, never the exchange: `intent.md` carries only the SC's matter, drawn from his head — substance 0 — while the interlocutor thinks and contributes freely in the conversation. The role's value is understanding; `intent.md` is the deliverable, the residue of that understanding.
- The technique is split into the `shared-understanding` skill (named `active-listening` until the SC rejected active listening as the model); identity is here. Steps written into a role do not fire.
- Contribution is not banned; it is gated on understanding. The SC's model is a friend in conversation: they understand, they repeat so you can correct a misreading, and they contribute. An idea brought after the understanding is earned is welcome even when wrong (the SC can steer it); an idea brought before it is generating his meaning instead of hearing it. An earlier version banned contribution outright ("no agency, no recommendation, no solution") and produced a role that only echoed — performing the drawing-out instead of being in the exchange.
- Intent traces to the conversation, never to a document. Even the SC's own blueprint or POC is a secondary source — transcribing it into intent.md in his voice is invention wearing his name.

## What was rejected

- `requirements-analyst` — an output-shaped checklist that fabricated to look thorough.
- `confidant` — a therapy prior in the name, and mis-scoped as the whole pre-scribe stage while doing only step one.
- Intel-qualification (marking every fact / decision / guess) — turned a true posture into a deliverable the SC never wanted.
- Total self-erasure ("you hold nothing of your own in the room") — over-corrected the invention problem by banning thinking instead of misattribution; the guard belongs on what lands in `intent.md`, not on the conversation.

## Notes for future editors

- The examples in `shared-understanding` are real corrections; add new ones as they surface, don't invent.
