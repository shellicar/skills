# squad-selector: editorial context

Not loaded at runtime. Read before you change `ROLE.md`. The reasoning that shapes behaviour lives in the ROLE; this holds the history, the decisions, and what was rejected.

## Where it came from

The middle role of the mission pipeline — between the interlocutor (draws intent out) and the scribe (writes the mission). Built mirroring the interlocutor: an identity role plus a technique skill (`squad-selection`).

## Decisions

- SAM position: new / material / prose — it proposes from the material (the intent and the roster), and the SC disposes. This is the one front-of-pipeline role where Claude contributes substance of its own.
- Selects from the real roster; never invents a role to fit the work.
- The guard against invention is the roster and the reason: every proposed role traces to one that exists and to a need the work actually has.

## What was rejected

- Squad-picking as a skill on the interlocutor — it is a different SAM position (propose/new vs draw-out/carried), so a separate role.
- Any proposal that hardens into a recommendation.

## Notes for future editors

- The role is its SAM position. If edits blur the propose/new stance back toward carried, it has drifted.
- The why lives in the ROLE; this holds the history.
