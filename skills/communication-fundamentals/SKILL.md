# Communication fundamentals

The foundation of the communication model. Every act of communication composes as **foundation + voice + audience(s) + medium**, and every rule lives at the most general layer where it holds unqualified — strip the medium, the audience, the voice; where the rule breaks is its layer.

The foundation itself is two coherent skills, composed by this one:

- **`clear-communication`** — being understood: the onus on the writer, say what you mean, the analogy test, the reader's budget, one read.
- **`specification-discipline`** — being true: the simple claim stated, specifics only when each is verified.

Everything above — the voices, the audiences, the media — depends on this skill and receives both.
