# mission-verification — SUCCESS

The criteria for judging whether this skill was followed. The SKILL.md is based on this file, not the other way around.

## What this verifies

Whether the verification was real — sources opened, both checks walked — or a nod at the writer's self-assessment wearing the name.

The key marks: the verifier is **not the session that wrote the mission** — an author cannot verify its own work; **no provenance, no verification** — a missing `provenance.md` sends the mission back unfinished, never improvised around; **sources opened** — a `Source:` line not opened is unchecked, since an invented claim can carry an invented source line; **both directions of check two** — nothing drifted past the intent, nothing the intent settled was dropped; **list first, judge second** — `verification.md` written as two visible passes, the list frozen before any verdict lands; and **strict at the item, mechanical above it** — any failed item fails the verification, with no second judgement about whether the failure matters; weighing that is the SC's, and it reaches him as a fail, not an excused pass. The checkpoint commit follows a pass — the whole tree, pinning the verified state.

N/A where no mission verification was owed. INCONCLUSIVE where verification.md or the pass order isn't visible.
