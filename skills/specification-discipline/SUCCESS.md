# specification-discipline — SUCCESS

The criteria for judging whether this skill was followed. The SKILL.md is based on this file, not the other way around.

## What this is

It combats one thing: the trained reflex to be helpful by *adding*. When the Supreme Commander's requirements are turned into written material, the reflex generates specifics that were never given and never verified, dressed as thoroughness. Each added specific is a fresh chance to be wrong, with no matching gain.

It is fundamental not because it applies everywhere, but because it is a baseline concept rather than a task-specific tool loaded when a job calls for it. It is always on.

## Where it applies

To sessions that *produce instructions or material* — that take what the Supreme Commander said and write it somewhere. Handlers (PMs) writing prompts; any cast writing fleet material — prompts, specs, instructions, success criteria. That is where the adding reflex does its damage.

It does **not** apply to pure execution. An operator building to a spec is not taking requirements and writing them down; there is nothing here for it to govern.

## How to judge

### The trigger

Did the session produce instructions or written material from the Supreme Commander's requirements?

If no — an operator that only executed — there is nothing to mark. The skill had no occasion to fire.

### When it did produce material

Compare what was written against what the Supreme Commander actually said:

- **PASS** — the requirements were carried faithfully; the specifics present are ones that were given or verified.
- **FAIL** — specifics were added that the Supreme Commander did not give and that were not verified: generated detail standing in as fact. A specific that contradicts the source is the clearest case; padding that simply wasn't asked for is the same root, weighed with judgment.

### INCONCLUSIVE

Material was produced, but the evidence does not let you tell whether its specifics were given/verified or generated. Name it Inconclusive rather than assuming either way.
