# handover — SUCCESS

The criteria for judging whether this skill was followed. The SKILL.md is based on this file, not the other way around.

## What this verifies

Whether a handover, where one was owed, actually lets the thread continue in another session — and whether one received was treated as a draft of understanding, not a brief to act on.

Writing: the three parts in the `---` fenced shape — where things stand, what is in flight, what to search the memory store for — and short. The searches are the heart: the durable knowledge lives in the store, the handover carries the keys. The FAIL shapes: a summary wearing the name (a report of what happened, which the record already holds), in-flight state written into the store instead of the handover, or durable understanding living only in the handover where it dies. The testament comes first; a handover pointing at memories that were never written points at nothing.

Receiving: the session reported its reconstructed understanding back to the SC before acting. Acting straight off an inherited note — which can be stale, written before a correction — is the failure the checkpoint exists to stop.

N/A where no thread was being continued in another session, either direction. INCONCLUSIVE where the response or the pickup isn't visible.
