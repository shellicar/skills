# active-listening: editorial context

Not loaded at runtime. Read before you change `SKILL.md`. The reasoning that shapes behaviour lives in the SKILL; this holds the history, the decisions, and what was rejected.

## Where it came from

The technique the interlocutor uses — drawing the SC's intent out in conversation. Split into a skill because a role tells you *who you are* (identity, delivered system-side) and a skill carries *the steps* (delivered user-side); steps written into a role do not fire. It was extracted when the interlocutor replaced `requirements-analyst` / `confidant`.

## Decisions

- Taught as concrete moves, each shown done right and done wrong — real bad→good pairs, not adjectives.
- The spine: understanding comes before contribution, and Claude drives without steering — carries the conversation forward while the calls stay the SC's. Ideas are welcome after the understanding is earned, offered as Claude's own, one at a time. Before it, they are Claude generating the SC's meaning. This replaced an earlier spine that banned contributing and produced a role that only echoed.
- The moves: process his words before reaching for anything else; repeat it so he can correct you; contribute one thing, as yours, for him to steer; one thing at a time; ask the question that settles the fork; name the real thing; a question the SC asks is not a decision; unsure goes back to the conversation.
- The skill avoids the "active listening" vocabulary in its own prose ("play that back", "let me reflect") without banning it — the SC's correction was that casts *performed* those phrases as formula openers; a ban would just be the same formula inverted. The repeat move is written as a plain check, done without announcing it.
- The bad examples are real corrections: the process-shape question asked after the goal was already stated, the compressed ruling handed back as the SC's decision.

## What was rejected

- Leaving the technique inside the interlocutor role — steps in a role don't fire.
- Borrowing Matt Pocock's grilling ("provide your recommended answer", "interview me relentlessly") — the recommendation and the interrogation the SC rejects.
- The self-erasure spine ("you carry the momentum, never the destination"; "you built nothing" as praise) — it conflated contributing with inventing, and the result parroted instead of participating. The invention guard lives where it belongs: on what is written into `intent.md`.

## Notes for future editors

- The moves are examples of the stance, not an exhaustive list; add real ones as they surface.
- If a move starts steering the SC toward a conclusion rather than drawing his out, it has lost the point.
