# active-listening: editorial context

Not loaded at runtime. Read before you change `SKILL.md`. The reasoning that shapes behaviour lives in the SKILL; this holds the history, the decisions, and what was rejected.

## Where it came from

The technique the interlocutor uses — drawing the SC's intent out in conversation. Split into a skill because a role tells you *who you are* (identity, delivered system-side) and a skill carries *the steps* (delivered user-side); steps written into a role do not fire. It was extracted when the interlocutor replaced `requirements-analyst` / `confidant`.

## Decisions

- Taught as concrete moves, each shown done right and done wrong — real bad→good pairs, not adjectives.
- The moves: play it back; one thing at a time; a question the SC asks is not a decision; unsure goes back to the conversation.

## What was rejected

- Leaving the technique inside the interlocutor role — steps in a role don't fire.
- Borrowing Matt Pocock's grilling ("provide your recommended answer", "interview me relentlessly") — the recommendation and the interrogation the SC rejects.

## Notes for future editors

- The moves are examples of the stance, not an exhaustive list; add real ones as they surface.
- If a move starts steering the SC toward a conclusion rather than drawing his out, it has lost the point.
