# prompt-authoring — SUCCESS

The criteria for judging whether this skill was followed. The SKILL.md is based on this file, not the other way around.

## What this verifies

Whether what reached the operator was written for a literal reader — because every vague or fabricated specific in a prompt lands in the code.

The key marks: **positive instructions with their reason** — "don't over-engineer" is a weak absence, and a rule without a reason becomes a rule to work around; **findings, not rules** — a finding carries its source and survives contact with reality, a rule traps the operator between obedience and the goal; **no role stance written** — what an operator is and produces comes from its block and `ROLE.md`, opened this session, and a sentence redefining a role traces to the SC's words in intent or does not appear; **scaffolded from the scripts**, never from an old prompt — prior prompts contaminate, and for the recurring recipes only the phase/role/skill skeleton carries across; and the pairing held — writing with `mission-grounding`, verifying with `mission-verification`; this skill alone produces an unfinished mission.

N/A where no prompt was written or updated. INCONCLUSIVE where the prompt or its composition isn't visible.
