The criteria for judging whether this skill was followed. The SKILL.md is based on this file, not the other way around.

## What this verifies

Whether a work item, where one was written, serves every reader on the page at once — the developer, the stakeholder, Stephen planning from it, a Claude implementing from it. That composition is what makes the medium.

The key marks: the title describes the effect or goal, so a stakeholder can weigh it and a developer can follow it into the code — a title naming a class or method is invisible to half its readers; the description is connected prose, a professional speaking to a colleague, not note fragments or a bullet dump (the opposite shape to a PR); and it says what without how — neither dev-only jargon nor dumbed down. The FAIL is text that serves one audience and abandons the rest.
