# mission-grounding — SUCCESS

The criteria for judging whether this skill was followed. The SKILL.md is based on this file, not the other way around.

## What this verifies

Whether the mission was composed from extracted evidence, in the right order — provenance first, mission from the rows and nothing else. The order is the whole skill: a trace written after the mission is a bibliography that approves its own author.

The key marks: `provenance.md` exists and came **before** `mission.md`; every Evidence row names one of the three sources only — SC, Project, Fleet — with the text it rests on (git inspection is not a source; a verified fact nobody decided is a gap sent upstream, never a row); every mission sentence traces to a row and every row is carried intact, in the intent's own words — additions are inventing, drops are losing what the SC decided; vehicles (`blueprint.md`, `investigation.md`) referenced, never transcribed into rows; Coverage rows for the goal and every objective — done by which phase, proven where; and gaps named and sent upstream, never filled.

The precision test on any composed line: could the operator reasonably choose differently? No means design leaked in without a row to carry it.
