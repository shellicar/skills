# squad-selection — SUCCESS

The criteria for judging whether this skill was followed. The SKILL.md is based on this file, not the other way around.

## What this verifies

Whether the squad reached the SC as a checkable proposal — the analysis carried, the call left with him.

The key marks: picks from the **real roster** — a role that does not exist is a gap surfaced, never invented; **every role carries its reason**, naming the need in the work it meets — explanation, not sales, and never instructions for the operator (a reason that redefines the role poisons the scribe downstream); **models and efforts** at the defaults — Opus for everything, Sonnet 5 undispatchable, supervisors fixed and not picked — with deviations argued to the SC, not taken; choices laid **along what the SC values**, the value elicited rather than assumed — a raw "A or B?" and a "use A" are the same failure from opposite ends; fake forks collapsed, real ones surfaced; and **no padding** — a role added because most missions have it is a role with no reason, and it comes out.

N/A where no squad was proposed. INCONCLUSIVE where the proposal isn't visible.
