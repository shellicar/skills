# Medium: mission

The reader is an operator cast (`audience-claude`) that runs the brief literally, start to finish, with no way back to the author. The craft, structure, and process are the fleet's skills — `prompt-authoring` for the writing, `mission-grounding` for the proof, `mission-artefacts` for what a mission is on disk; this file places the mission in the communication model.

**Everything in it lands as work.** A finding lands as a starting point; a rule lands as obedience, including when wrong; an ambiguity lands as an unpredictable interpretation. When something goes wrong, look at what was written.

**It stands alone.** The operator reads this file and the mission's referenced vehicles, nothing upstream. Decided content is carried whole, in the deciding voice's words; artefacts with their own authors (blueprint, investigation) are referenced, never transcribed.

**The goal opens it.** Every phase serves the goal, and the goal is what the operator recovers to when a precise instruction breaks on contact.
